# Master of Trade – Learning Resources

## 1. Candlestick Patterns
- **Definition**: Visual representation of price movement over a specific period, used to predict future direction.
- **Key Patterns**:
  - **Bullish Engulfing**: Small bearish candle followed by larger bullish candle that fully engulfs it.
  - **Hammer**: Small body with long lower shadow, signals potential upward reversal.
  - **Shooting Star**: Inverse of Hammer; long upper shadow, signals potential downward reversal.
  - **Doji**: Very small body, indicates indecision; significant after strong trends.
  - **Morning Star / Evening Star**: Three‑candle pattern indicating reversal.
- **References**:
  - [MQL5 – Candlestick Patterns](https://www.mql5.com/en/analysis/candlesticks)
  - [ForexFactory – Candlestick Basics](https://www.forexfactory.com/education/candlestick-patterns)

## 2. Chart Patterns
- **Definition**: Repeating formations on price charts that traders use to anticipate future moves.
- **Common Patterns**:
  - **Head & Shoulders**: Signals trend reversal.
  - **Double Top / Double Bottom**: Indicates reversal after testing a level twice.
  - **Triangles** (Ascending, Descending, Symmetrical): Continuation or breakout patterns.
  - **Flags & Pennants**: Short‑term continuation after a strong move.
  - **Rectangles**: Range‑bound area acting as support/resistance.
- **References**:
  - [Bloomberg – Chart Patterns Overview](https://www.bloomberg.com/markets/patterns)
  - [Reuters – Technical Chart Patterns](https://www.reuters.com/Investing/chart-patterns)

## 3. Multi‑Timeframe Analysis
- **Why Use It**: Confirms trend strength and entry timing by aligning higher‑timeframe trend with lower‑timeframe signals.
- **Typical Workflow**:
  1. Identify trend on a higher TF (e.g., H4 or D1).
  2. Look for entry signal on a lower TF (e.g., M15, M30).
  3. Check alignment of indicators across TFs.
- **References**:
  - [FXStreet – Multi‑Timeframe Analysis Guide](https://www.fxstreet.com/education/multi-timeframe-analysis)

## 4. Cross‑Pair Analysis
- **Concept**: Examine correlated currency pairs to infer market dynamics when the primary pair is thinly traded or to spot arbitrage opportunities.
- **Key Points**:
  - Positive correlation (e.g., EUR/USD & GBP/USD) often move together.
  - Divergence can signal weakening momentum.
  - Use correlation matrices to monitor shifting relationships.
- **References**:
  - [MQL5 – Correlation Analysis](https://www.mql5.com/en/analysis/correlation)
  - [ForexFactory – Correlation Strategy](https://www.forexfactory.com/education/correlation-strategy)

## 5. Convergence & Divergence
- **Convergence**: Momentum indicators (RSI, MACD) move in the same direction as price, reinforcing the trend.
- **Divergence**: Indicators move opposite to price, warning of potential reversal.
  - **RSI Divergence**: Price makes higher highs but RSI makes lower highs (bearish) or vice‑versa (bullish).
  - **MACD Divergence**: Similar concept; often used with histogram crossovers.
- **Trading Tips**:
  - Combine with support/resistance for higher probability setups.
  - Use higher timeframe divergence for stronger signals.
- **References**:
  - [Investopedia – RSI Divergence](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/r/rsi.asp)
  - [FXStreet – MACD Divergence](https://www.fxstreet.com/technical-analysis/macd-divergence)

## 6. Scalping Trading
- **Characteristics**:
  - Very short holding periods (seconds to minutes).
  - Focus on small price moves, high trade frequency.
  - Requires tight spreads, low latency execution.
- **Typical Indicators/Tools**:
  - **EMA 8 & EMA 21** for trend direction.
  - **Stochastic** or **RSI** for overbought/oversold zones.
  - **Level 2** order book data for micro‑price moves.
- **Risk Management**:
  - Use tight stop‑loss (e.g., 5‑10 pips).
  - Keep risk per trade ≤ 0.5 % of equity.
  - Trade only during high‑liquidity sessions (e.g., London‑New York overlap).
- **References**:
  - [BabyPips – Scalping Strategies](https://www.babypips.com/learn/scalping)
  - [TradingView – Scalping Ideas](https://www.tradingview.com/scalping-strategies)
